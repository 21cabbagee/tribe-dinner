# TribeDinner: Curated High-Niche Micro-Salons & Private Dinners

> **Algorithmic guest-matched private micro-salons and intimate dinners for founders, executives, and operators. Zero noisy conferences. 100% peer density.**

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Thesis
- **Product Name:** TribeDinner
- **One-Liner:** Curated private 8-seat dinner salons connecting high-impact founders and operators facing the exact same operational bottlenecks, with algorithmic seat-matching and ticket escrow.
- **Problem Thesis:** Founders and tech executives regularly waste thousands attending 500-person tech conferences and sponsor-choked networking dinners where real conversations are impossible. TribeDinner unbundles business networking into intimate, hyper-niche 8-seat dinners held in private rooms, speakeasies, and lofts under the Chatham House Rule.
- **Bottoms-Up TAM:** 50,000 metro clusters × 50 dinners/year × $300 spread = **$750 Million TAM**.

---

## 2. In-Scope Core Features (Black Car 12-Hour MVP Scope)
1. **Algorithmic Peer Matching:** Intake engine clusters guests by ARR stage, technical stack, and active operational bottleneck (e.g. AI inference compute, Service-as-a-Software unbundling, cross-border tax).
2. **Strict 8-Seat Density:** Every table is hard-capped at 8 guests to guarantee intimacy, raw vulnerability, and meaningful connections.
3. **Zero-Inventory Private Dining Coordination:** Drop-serviced private dining room partnerships across San Francisco (SoMa), New York (SoHo), Austin, and Boston.
4. **Escrow-Protected Ticket Reservations:** Transparent ticket purchasing protecting both guests and host curators.
